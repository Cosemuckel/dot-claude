# Research protocol

1. Define the exact facts needed for the downstream decision.
2. Do the core bounded investigation directly when practical.
3. Split only genuinely independent or context-heavy questions.
4. Use `micro-reader` or a focused research worker when isolation materially reduces context or enables safe parallelism.
5. Use Context7 only when current, version-specific library/framework documentation is relevant.
6. Distinguish observed facts from external claims and assumptions.
7. Stop when the evidence is sufficient for the decision.
8. For audit/diagnosis work, do not force investigation toward a plan.

Return a compact research packet: findings, exact evidence locations/sources, material constraints, and unresolved
uncertainty only.
